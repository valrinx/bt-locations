# BT Locations

## Register

product

## Users

Field operators and coordinators use BT Locations on phones and desktop browsers
to find, add, correct, import, and recover location records. Every visitor signs
in or creates an account before opening the map. New members begin with read-only
Viewer access, approved collaborators receive narrowly scoped data-management
permissions, and the owner controls access.

## Product Purpose

BT Locations is the shared operational map and source-of-truth interface for
location data stored in Supabase. Success means the map remains quick to open
after authentication, recoverable after mistakes, and protected against
unauthorized access or changes.

## Brand Personality

Operational, compact, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a focused
field tool whose controls are predictable under time pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page styling, decorative dashboard patterns, playful security
flows, hidden permission states, and interactions that rely on hover or visual
color alone.

## Design Principles

1. Require authentication before map access and keep sign-in straightforward.
2. Make authorization status explicit before a destructive action.
3. Enforce every permission in the database, not only in the interface.
4. Preserve the established dense field-tool interaction language.
5. Prefer recoverable operations and clear audit ownership.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, readable status text, touch
targets suitable for mobile use, reduced-motion compatibility, and semantic
labels that do not communicate state through color alone.
